Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of what ResumeIQ stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store and why

DataWhy
Account (name, email, bcrypt password hash)To sign you in. We never store your password itself.
Profile & career preferencesTo personalise job matching, gaps and roadmaps.
Uploaded resume files and their extracted textTo analyse, compare versions, and let you download them. Stored privately on the server with random filenames, never publicly accessible.
Analyses, job descriptions you paste, cover letters, interview answersSo you can reopen history and track progress.
Applications, saved jobs, learning progress, notificationsTo run the tracker, analytics and reminders.
Operational logs (request path, status, latency, error type)Reliability and security. Logs never contain passwords, API keys, tokens or resume content.
AI usage counts and approximate token totalsCost control and abuse prevention.

AI processing

When AI features are enabled, relevant text (for example resume text or a job description) is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce structured analysis. We instruct the model not to invent facts, validate every response against a schema, and fall back to deterministic analysis when AI is unavailable. Scores are computed by our own transparent rubric, not by the AI.

Job data

Job listings come from public job APIs (Remotive, Arbeitnow, and Adzuna when configured). We link to the original posting and do not create listings.

Who can see your data

Only you. Administrators see aggregate statistics (counts, usage, error rates) and cannot view resume content through the application.

Your controls

  • Delete a resume - My Resumes → Delete (removes the file and its versions).
  • Delete analysis history - Analysis History or Settings → Privacy & data.
  • Export your data - Settings → Privacy & data → Export (JSON).
  • Delete your account - Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, files and all associated records.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use short-lived tokens in HTTP-only cookies with CSRF protection. Uploads are validated by extension and file signature, size-limited, stored outside any public directory, and never executed.
